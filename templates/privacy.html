Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

01

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, along with the expense records, categories, amounts, and dates you log while using the Service. We may also collect basic technical data, like browser type and access times, to help keep the Service secure and reliable.

02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the core features of Spendly — logging expenses, generating spending summaries, and keeping your account secure. We may also use it to communicate important updates about the Service, respond to support requests, and improve the features we offer over time.

03

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

04

Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions like hosting or infrastructure, who process data only as needed to support the Service and are bound to keep it confidential. We do not share your expense data with advertisers.

05

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to access, update, or delete your data, please reach out to us through your account settings or the support channel listed on our website.
